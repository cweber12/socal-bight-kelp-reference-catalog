  "The survey area for the 2018 Southern California Bight Regional Monitoring Program (Bight’18)
  spanned from Point Conception, CA in the north to the US-Mexico border in the south and from the
  mainland coastal embayments west to the Channel Islands (Figure 1). The soft sediment portions of
  this region less than 1,000 m deep were divided into eleven strata based upon known biogeographic
  breaks in community composition (e.g., estuaries or deep continental shelf) or area of different
  regulatory/management interest (e.g., ports or continental slope) (Table 1)." Table 1, which its
  caption says gives "the range of depth at which those stations were located", states "7-30" for
  the "Inner Shelf" stratum, under a column header reading "Depth Range" over the unit "(m)".
  "Sediment samples for benthic macrofauna analysis were collected from July 1 to September 28,
  2018." "Samples of benthic

  Southern California Bight (6-200 m deep) and embayments. These areas represent approximately 36%

  condition over the last 20 years (1998-2018)".
